Common Causes of TikTok Shadowbans

Understanding why shadowbans happen is the first step to fixing them

Regional Mismatch

This is the #1 cause of shadowbans that most creators don't know about. If you're creating content for a US audience but your account was created or is being accessed from outside the US, TikTok's algorithm will limit your reach.

TikTok prioritizes showing content to users in the same region. When there's a mismatch between your account's location and your target audience, the algorithm flags your account as "low relevance" and reduces your visibility.

The Solution:

Use a US-based TikTok account that's verified to be in the United States. This ensures your content reaches American audiences without regional restrictions.

Violating Community Guidelines

Posting content that violates TikTok's community guidelines (spam, inappropriate content, copyright violations) can result in a shadowban. Even if your content doesn't get removed, repeated violations can trigger algorithmic penalties.

Rapid Growth or Engagement Spikes

Sudden spikes in followers, views, or engagement can trigger TikTok's spam detection. The algorithm may temporarily limit your account to verify that the growth is organic and not from bots or purchased engagement.

Using VPNs or esims

Accessing TikTok through VPNs or esims can trigger security flags. TikTok's algorithm detects location inconsistencies and may shadowban accounts that frequently change locations or use masking services.

Repetitive Content or Hashtag Stuffing

Posting very similar content repeatedly or using the same hashtags excessively can be flagged as spam. TikTok's algorithm favors diverse, engaging content that provides value to viewers.
